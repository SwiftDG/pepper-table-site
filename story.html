Pepper Table / Our idea

Make room
for the meal.

Pepper Table imagines a restaurant where the menu is easy to find and familiar food gets the attention it deserves.

01 / The food

Why this concept exists

Start with what someone came to see.

A guest looking for a place to eat usually wants the menu, the food and the practical details without a long search. This concept leads with the dishes. It gives each one a clear name, a plain description and a photo when one helps.

The imagined menu borrows from Nigerian staples such as jollof, egusi, grilled fish and plantain. The site is a design exercise, so it does not invent an address, hours, prices or a booking system.
